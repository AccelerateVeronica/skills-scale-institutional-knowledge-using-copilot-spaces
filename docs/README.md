# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through a customer-first, iterative lifecycle with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous learning.

## Project Management Processes

Projects move through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Initiation validates the need, desired outcomes, stakeholders, and whether the work should proceed. Planning defines scope, backlog, estimates, dependencies, risks, and milestones. During execution, teams deliver and test small increments, review progress regularly, and communicate blockers. Before release, the team verifies readiness, deploys, communicates the change, and prepares rollback or mitigation steps. Retrospectives capture lessons and track improvement actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers own outcomes, priorities, and success measures; and delivery teams build and validate the work. Risks, dependencies, and stakeholder updates are monitored throughout the lifecycle.
