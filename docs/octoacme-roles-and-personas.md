# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead engineering delivery, align team capacity with project commitments, and support the health and effectiveness of the engineering team.

### Responsibilities
- Plan engineering capacity and surface staffing or skill constraints
- Coordinate execution across developers and engineering leads
- Track engineering dependencies, delivery risks, and escalations
- Support team health, sustainable delivery, and continuous improvement

### Decision Rights
- Own engineering capacity allocation and execution planning with engineering leads
- Recommend delivery trade-offs when scope, capacity, or technical dependencies change
- Escalate technical decisions outside the team's authority to the appropriate owner

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers on plans, dependencies, delivery forecasts, and escalations
- Align with Product Managers on priority changes and scope trade-offs
- Guide Developers on execution and remove team-level delivery blockers
- Give stakeholders clear updates on engineering capacity, risks, and commitments

### Goals
- Establish realistic, sustainable engineering commitments
- Keep engineering work aligned with product outcomes
- Resolve delivery obstacles early

### Typical Communication
- Capacity and delivery planning with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Engineering team check-ins and escalation updates
- Delivery forecasts and dependency reviews

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define how a project verifies product quality and whether the evidence is sufficient for release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Set the test strategy, coverage expectations, and quality gates
- Coordinate validation across test environments and workflows
- Track defects, test results, and unresolved quality risks
- Assess release readiness and communicate gaps

### Decision Rights
- Own test strategy and quality criteria in coordination with Developers and Product Managers
- Recommend release readiness based on test evidence and agreed quality gates
- Escalate unmet quality gates; release approval follows the project's release governance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers on testability, defect triage, and fixes
- Work with Product Managers to verify acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Keep Project Managers informed of test progress, quality risks, and readiness
- Include stakeholders in validation when their workflows or acceptance are affected

### Goals
- Detect important defects before release
- Make quality and release risks visible
- Provide consistent, evidence-based readiness assessments

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, defect triage, and quality reports
- Release-readiness reviews with Project Managers, Product Managers, and Developers

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads identify security, privacy, and regulatory obligations and help the team meet required controls throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs, data handling, and changes for security and compliance risks
- Define required controls, reviews, and evidence with relevant specialists
- Track risk mitigations, exceptions, and compliance obligations
- Advise teams on secure implementation and release controls

### Decision Rights
- Set or interpret security and compliance requirements within their delegated authority
- Escalate unacceptable risks and require review or remediation under applicable policy
- Route formal risk acceptance to the designated accountable owner

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers to plan reviews, track mitigations, and escalate risks
- Advise Product Managers on security or compliance implications of product choices
- Work with Developers on secure design, implementation, and remediation
- Coordinate with stakeholders and control owners on obligations and evidence

### Goals
- Address security and compliance risks early
- Make control ownership and exceptions traceable
- Support releases that meet applicable policy and regulatory requirements

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews, control checklists, and remediation tracking
- Security or compliance findings shared with delivery leads and stakeholders

---

## Customer Success / Support Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Partners bring customer feedback and service operations needs into planning, launch readiness, and post-release follow-up.

### Responsibilities
- Summarize customer feedback, support trends, and recurring issues
- Identify customer impact and support needs for planned changes
- Coordinate support readiness, knowledge articles, and escalation guidance
- Monitor adoption concerns and post-release feedback

### Decision Rights
- Own support readiness recommendations and customer-impact assessments
- Identify launch risks from service capacity, known issues, or support gaps
- Provide customer evidence to inform prioritization; Product Managers retain product priority decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Share customer needs and adoption signals with Product Managers
- Coordinate launch tasks, support dependencies, and escalations with Project Managers
- Work with Developers and QA Leads to understand known issues and troubleshooting steps
- Keep stakeholders informed about customer impact and support readiness

### Goals
- Prepare support teams and customers for change
- Reduce avoidable customer disruption after release
- Ensure customer feedback informs future improvements

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and support trend reports
- Launch readiness check-ins and post-release issue reviews

---

## Data / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Owners define how project outcomes are measured and ensure teams can trust the data used to assess impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define metric specifications, baselines, and measurement plans
- Coordinate instrumentation and analytics requirements with engineering
- Validate data quality and prepare dashboards or outcome reports
- Track results against agreed success criteria after release

### Decision Rights
- Own metric definitions and analytics methodology for the agreed measurement plan
- Flag data quality limitations and distinguish measured outcomes from assumptions
- Recommend changes to instrumentation; Product Managers own product success criteria and priorities

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to define measurable outcomes and interpret results
- Work with Project Managers to include measurement tasks and reporting milestones in plans
- Coordinate with Developers on instrumentation and with QA Leads on validating events and data flows
- Present evidence and limitations to stakeholders for outcome reviews

### Goals
- Make project impact measurable and understandable
- Improve confidence in decisions based on product data
- Identify where outcomes differ from expectations

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions, dashboards, and data-quality notes
- Outcome reviews with Product Managers, Project Managers, and stakeholders
