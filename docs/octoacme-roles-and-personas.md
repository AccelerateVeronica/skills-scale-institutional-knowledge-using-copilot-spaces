# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead delivery execution, team health, and technical planning. They turn priorities into sustainable delivery practices while supporting engineers and aligning work with project commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Own team capacity, sprint execution, and delivery forecasting
- Coordinate with the Product Manager and Project Manager on scope trade-offs and sequencing
- Support engineering planning, dependency management, and risk mitigation
- Coach engineers, monitor workload, and improve team efficiency and quality
- Escalate technical blockers and staffing constraints to the appropriate stakeholders

### Goals
- Deliver predictable and sustainable execution
- Improve team clarity, productivity, and accountability
- Reduce bottlenecks and technical debt caused by unclear ownership

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering planning and delivery reviews
- Dependency and blocker updates with PMs and stakeholders
- Coaching conversations, team health discussions, and staffing decisions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers on execution quality and team health
- Partners with Project Managers on schedule, risk, and escalation tracking
- Aligns with Product Managers on roadmap sequencing and trade-offs

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and govern quality standards, validation strategies, and release readiness. They ensure that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and are fit for production use.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release readiness criteria
- Coordinate test planning across unit, integration, and end-to-end coverage
- Partner with engineering and product teams on acceptance criteria and validation
- Track defects, regressions, and test evidence before release
- Escalate quality or validation risks that could affect launch decisions

### Goals
- Increase confidence in product quality and release readiness
- Reduce defect leakage and avoid preventable production issues
- Make quality ownership visible across the delivery process

### Typical Communication
- QA planning, test status reviews, and release signoff meetings
- Defect triage with engineering and product leads
- Product and stakeholder updates during milestone validation

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers on testability, bug triage, and regression coverage
- Supports Project Managers with project readiness and release checkpoints
- Coordinates with Product Managers to verify that outcomes and acceptance criteria are met

---

## Security & Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Leads protect the organization from technical and regulatory risk by guiding secure design, controls, and operational readiness across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, data handling, and implementation choices for security risks
- Define security checkpoints, compliance requirements, and control evidence
- Partner with teams on privacy, access, auditability, and incident response readiness
- Assess project-level risk and guide mitigations before deployment or release
- Escalate critical vulnerabilities, policy gaps, or compliance blockers

### Goals
- Reduce operational and regulatory exposure
- Ensure secure delivery without slowing value creation unnecessarily
- Improve trust and resilience in project outputs

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews, security design discussions, and release checks
- Incident response coordination and compliance follow-ups
- Security signoff and stakeholder briefings for high-risk initiatives

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers to validate secure implementation choices
- Advises Project Managers and Product Managers on risk, controls, and release gates
- Collaborates with Stakeholders during reviews of policy, privacy, and business impact

---

## Customer Success / Support Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Partners ensure teams understand post-launch realities, customer pain points, and service quality. They connect product delivery to customer outcomes and support readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, support trends, and issue patterns
- Highlight adoption challenges, service gaps, and post-release risks
- Coordinate with product and engineering on customer-impacting bugs or release concerns
- Help define onboarding, support readiness, and escalation paths for new features
- Provide insight into customer impact and business outcomes after release

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce friction between delivery teams and service operations
- Ensure customer needs are reflected in prioritization and release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback loops, issue reviews, and service health updates
- Cross-functional discussions on support readiness and launch implications
- Stakeholder updates on customer impact and product reliability

### Interaction with existing roles
- Feeds Product Managers with customer insights and market signal
- Supports Project Managers with release communication and operational readiness
- Partners with Developers to triage recurring issues and prioritize fixes

---

## Data & Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data & Analytics Owners provide measurement, instrumentation, and reporting expertise so teams can evaluate whether outcomes are being achieved and where improvements are needed.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation plans, and dashboards
- Validate whether project outputs are meeting agreed business and user outcomes
- Partner with product and PM teams on reporting and decision-quality data
- Identify data quality, trend, or adoption issues that affect delivery decisions
- Support post-release evaluation and continuous improvement activities

### Goals
- Make outcomes measurable and actionable
- Improve learning loops across planning, execution, and release stages
- Ensure decisions are based on evidence rather than assumptions

### Typical Communication
- Measurement planning, KPI reviews, and retrospective follow-ups
- Reporting to stakeholders on impact, adoption, and quality signals
- Data-driven discussions with product, PM, and engineering leads

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Product Managers with outcome measurement and prioritization evidence
- Helps Project Managers track milestone health, risk signals, and performance trends
- Enables Developers and QA teams to validate quality and feature adoption through telemetry

---

## Why additional personas matter

As projects grow in complexity, a small set of core roles is not always enough to cover delivery, risk, quality, support, and outcomes. Adding these personas clarifies accountability, reduces handoff gaps, and creates clearer decision-making paths across the project lifecycle. This improves planning quality, helps teams respond to risk earlier, and strengthens the connection between delivery work and business value.
